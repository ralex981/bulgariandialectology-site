Huhla 2: 75

Full Line:
učìlištetu ə tàm če s'ètne nəpràihə tvà sià nəpràihə tvà
Cyrillic full line:
учѝлиштету ə та̀м че с’ѐтне нəпра̀иhə тва̀ сиа̀ нəпра̀иhə тва̀
Translation:
… the school. Over there. And later they built this one. They built this one [just] recently.
