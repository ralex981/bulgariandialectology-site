Stikŭl 3: 91

Full Line:
ut nàštə imɛ̀še imɛ̀ edìn čuvɛ̀k ud nàšɤte ta utìd tə gu vìd’e
Cyrillic full line:
ут на̀штə име̂̀ше име̂̀ едѝн чуве̂̀к уд на̀шъте та утѝд тə гу вѝд’е
Translation:
of our [people], there was this one man of ours [who] went and saw him, [and he was]
