Gorno Vŭršilo 1: 54

Full Line:
nèma po restoràntite kàk vìe sɤ̀ga nìe domà
Cyrillic full line:
нèма по ресторàнтите кàк вѝе съ̀га нѝе домà
Translation:
not in restaurants the way you [do it] now. We [do it] at home.
