Baskalci 2: 28

Full Line:
na lèvoto ušè ti e belègo odzàt ràbuška nalì odzàt se vìka
Cyrillic full line:
на лѐвото ушѐ ти е белѐго оѕа̀т ра̀бушка налѝ оѕа̀т се вѝка
Translation:
And on the left ear you’ve got a mark, a notch in the back. It’s called “the back”
