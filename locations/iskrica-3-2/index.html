Iskrica 3: 4

Full Line:
i kàktu stənà səmìčək vèči sìčkiti si utìdaa ut tòu sv’àt
Cyrillic full line:
и ка̀кту стəна̀ сəмѝчəк вѐчи сѝчкити си утѝдаа ут то̀у св’а̀т
Translation:
As it turned out, I’m alone [now]; all of them have already departed this world.
