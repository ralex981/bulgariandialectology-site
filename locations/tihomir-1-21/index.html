Tihomir 1: 150

Full Line:
i pɔ̀strime gɯ tač'ème gᵚi pàk s etò as kač'ɔ̀
Cyrillic full line:
и по̂̀стриме гъ̴ тач'ѐме гᵚи па̀к с ето̀ ас кач'о̂̀
Translation:
and make the colors varied. We weave them, the same way, with a shuttle.
