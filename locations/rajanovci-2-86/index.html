Rajanovci 2: 88

Full Line:
dà ìmaše ednò onì ga sàdešeu i vàrešeu slàtko od n’èga
Cyrillic full line:
да̀ ѝмаше едно̀ онѝ га са̀дешеу и ва̀решеу сла̀тко од н’ѐга
Translation:
Yes. There was one, they planted it and then cooked sweets from it.
