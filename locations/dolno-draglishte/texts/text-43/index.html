1 (b) čùkna: kəmbànite ìdeme v čèrkvə vrɤ̀nemè se ut tàm

The church bells ring, and we go to church. [When] we come back from there,

2 (b) hàjde nə tɤpənètu tùpa: č' ìim nə òrutu nə rekàtə

it's off to the drums, they’re drumming! And we’ll go to the horo at the river.

3 (b) tə òru tə òru təkà se e raptìlu ednò vrème

And dance, dance – that’s what people did in the old days.

4 (b) stròexme jəjcàtə pà sme pà sme càl' pos pustìli

We painted eggs [for Easter], and we kept the entire [Lenten] fast.

5 (b) tə sme udìli də kòpəme də nəpràime pàte tàm də rəbòtime

And we went to dig, in order to make a road, to work there

6 (b) kəə rəzlòšku kəə gòdlesku pàte tə kòpəme čəkɤ̀l' tàm

on the road [going] towards Razlog and Godlevo. We dig gravel there,

7 (b) tə òdeme sus merudìjə s rəsòl kərtòfč'i kòpəme tàm càl' dè̝n

and we go with mint, sauerkraut and potatoes [for lunch]. We dig there all day,

8 (b) rəbòtame sè s pòsnu jàdene sètne vèče kət dòe pà velìjden

work all day [just] with Lenten food. And then when Easter comes,

9 (b) vèče pà se bujədìsa: červèni jàjca pà se ublàžime

everyone has been dyeing eggs red, and we break the fast.

10 (b) kòj si nèštu zəkòle òdejme po gòste

Somebody slaughters an animal, and everybody goes visiting.

11 (b) u kùmuvètu č' ìiš nə gòste č' ìda: kumìcətə u kùmuvètu

You’ll go visit your godparents, the goddaughter [and her people] will go to the godparents

12 (b) tàm vèče se jàgne zəkòl'e jadè se pìe se

[where] they’re already slaughtering a lamb. You eat, you drink,

13 (b) pà se òru ìgrə è təkà bèše

you dance the horo [some more]. So that’s how it was.
