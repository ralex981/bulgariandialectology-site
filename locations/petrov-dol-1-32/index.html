Petrov Dol 1: 34

Full Line:
vìkɤ tì mi dàdi ə jɤvrèn’č’e às ti dàɤm xristijànči tɤrkàl’emi i
Cyrillic full line:
вѝкъ тѝ ми да̀ди ə йъврѐн’ч’е а̀с ти да̀ъм христийа̀нчи търка̀л’еми и
Translation:
says, “You gave me a little Jew and I now give you a little Christian.” We roll it
