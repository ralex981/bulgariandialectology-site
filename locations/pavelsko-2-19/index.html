Pavelsko 2: 12

Full Line:
t’è wòd’ehə səs ufč’ɛ̀re ufc’ène i š’ ìdeme gòr’e nə kəšlʌ̀tə
Cyrillic full line:
т’ѐ ўо̀д’еhә сәс уфч’е̂̀ре уфц’ѐне и ш’ ѝдеме го̀р’е нә кәшлъ̂̀тә
Translation:
They go off with the shepherds – the sheep [that is], and we’ll go up to the sheepfold.
