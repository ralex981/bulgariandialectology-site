1 (c) segà decàta tìja decà nèka ti kažème

Kids today, these kids – let us tell [about our times].

2 (c) od dvanàese godìni sam počnàla xlèb da mèsa ednò vrème

I started making bread when I was twelve years old. [That was] the old days.

3 (c) ne è kat seà da ni donòsat xlèp mesùvahme si domàšen

Not like now when they bring us [ready-made] bread. We made our own, home-made.

4 (c) ìmame ednò stòlče nòšvi si ìmame takvìja drɤ̀veni tàm si sè stojàa
