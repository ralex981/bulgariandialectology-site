Huhla 1: 25

Full Line:
du èj tùvə səm òd'lə bòsə nə učìlište nìšto mi n'è e
Cyrillic full line:
ду ѐй ту̀вə сəм о̀д’лə бо̀сə нə учѝлиште нѝшто ми н’ѐ е
Translation:
And I came barefoot all the way to here, to school. I had no problem with that.
