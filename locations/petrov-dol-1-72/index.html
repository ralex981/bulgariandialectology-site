Petrov Dol 1: 74

Full Line:
dunàs’ə jnà rìza d’èt i namìrə pr’ekàrvət i du trì pɤ̀t’ə
Cyrillic full line:
дуна̀с’ə йна̀ рѝза д’ѐт и намѝрə пр’ека̀рвəт и ду трѝ пъ̀т’ə
Translation:
who found it – brings a shift, and they pull it through the shift three times.
