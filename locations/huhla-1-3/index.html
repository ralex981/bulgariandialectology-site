Huhla 1: 12

Full Line:
m'ètl'en m'i d'èt mitɤ̀t šə stɤ̀ržene ustɤ̀rgəni
Cyrillic full line:
м’ѐтл’ен м’и д’ѐт митъ̀т шə стъ̀ржене устъ̀ргəни
Translation:
[No, bread] from a broom. What you sweep with. We scrape [it], scrape [it] up.
