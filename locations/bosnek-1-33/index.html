Bosnek 1: 31

Full Line:
ednìte pa na plèvata koì mràzime decà gi tùrime
Cyrillic full line:
еднѝте па на плѐвата коѝ мра̀зиме деца̀ ги ту̀риме
Translation:
and others would be down with the chaff. We put the kids we hated [down there]
