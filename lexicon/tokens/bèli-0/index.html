bèli

Meaning:
white
Lexeme:
бял
Other:
adj
Number:
pl
Linguistic trait:
jat root s1 pal1 c0 /e/

Lines where bèli appears

  • Bosnek 3: 49 - i nalì sɤz bèli kɤ̀rpi nalì znàeš kakvò se klàn’axa
