anecdotes

Huhla 2

80 (a) n'e n'e ud drùgəd'e duhòdəə gòn'iə sə tùkə

No, no, they came from somewhere else. There was hanky-panky here.

81 (a) ədìn b'àše dušɤ̀l če s ədnɤ̀ ə prijàtelkə če nìe ut'ỳvəne

One had come, and he [had] a girlfriend. So we went

82 (a) də sə učìne tè s prijàtelkətə vɤ̀tre nìe ut'ỳvəne kàzvəne

in to learn, and he was inside with his girlfriend. [So] we went to tell

83 (a) nə km'ètə dòjde km'ètə zəgòn' g'i nikulàj [unclear] nikulàj [unclear]

the mayor, and the mayor came and went for them. “Nikolay!” [unclear] “Nikolay!” [unclear]

84 (a) b'àgət tùkə nətàtək [laughter] èj təkà ednà ubɤ̀rkənə a

And they ran off, back [to where they'd come from]. [laughter] Oh, such a mess.
