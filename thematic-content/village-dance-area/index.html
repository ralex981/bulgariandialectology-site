village dance area

Drabišna 1

65 (a) də zəp'àete kət vàs ìkəm təkɤ̀f hùbəu gul'àm pluštàt ìmə

"You should sing. If we were you," I said, "[with] such a big nice square –

66 (a) nə nàž gudìni əku ìməši tòu pluštàt ìkəm kɤ̀vi hurà

If there’d been such a square in our days," I said, "what circle dances

67 (a) št'àhme də igràeme pək nìe f t'àə bətàci f t'àə ròkli

we’d have danced!" But we [danced] in that swamp and in these clothes –

68 (a) pl'às pl'às t'àə mɤ̀ški̥ t'àə mòmč'etə pl'às pl'às

[still] clap clap!, The men, the boys: clap, clap!

70 (a) i tò təkà nə čàjə nə tòu čàj tùkə b'àš'e

All there in the river[bed]. There, where it used to be a river,

Vasiljovo 2

28 (b) na n’àkakvi obròci se sabìra:me ta si igràe:me

We’d gather together at certain ritual spots outside the village, and dance.
