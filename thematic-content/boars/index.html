boars

Dolna Sekirna 3

78 (a) ama divè svìn’e sṛ̀ne zàjci e tekòva ama pòveče

Ah, wild boar, deer, rabbits, and such. But mostly

79 (a) divè svìn’e i zàjci tèpaju

they would kill wild boar and rabbits.

81 (a) òn sàmo zàek edèše ə mòjɤt stàrɤc dìp ot svin’ù ne edè

He only ate rabbit. My husband never ate a bit of wild boar meat,

82 (a) ama tèpa što izlèzne

but he would kill [one] if it emerged.

83 (VZh) à da ne prài pàkos da ne da ne ròvi

So they wouldn’t do damage, so they wouldn’t burrow

84 (VZh) u tovà u kòmpirɤt

among the potatoes.

85 (a) dà dà ə ìmaemo ednò kùče vìxɤr štò izlèzne

Yes, yes! We had a dog [named] “Whirlwind”. When he went out

86 (a) sɤs kùčeto svin’ù če otèpa

with the dog, he’d kill a boar [each time].

Stakevci 4

18 (f) jà mìšo čùjem če dùmu svin’ète i jedù

Hey Misho, I hear they say the [wild] pigs are eating them.
