flax

Sŭrnica 2

10 (a) dud’è puupr’əd’èš’ l’ənɤ̀ dud’è puutəč’èš’ dud’è si nəpràviš’ plàtnu

until you spin up all the flax, do all the weaving, make fabric

16 (b) təkɤ̀t dumàšnu plətnò

They weave homemade fabric.

19 (b) ud l’èn

From flax.

21 (a) òti ž’enìte l’ənɤ̀

Because the women [worked] the flax.

22 (MM) znàči lenɤ̀ e stàval tùka ràždal se e

So flax grew here. It took root here.

25 (a) mnògu ùbəf l’ɤ̀n stàvəš’e

Really nice flax grew here.

26 (MM) a segà vèče slàgate li nè

Do you still plant it? Or not?

27 (b) əˀə

Nope.

32 (a) š’i pòčnət nɛ̀kuj pà də sejɤ̀t

Some people will [certainly] start to sow it again.
