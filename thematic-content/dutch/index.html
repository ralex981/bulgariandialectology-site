Dutch

Bosnek 1

133 (a) i u xolàndija kato ìdexme ne ìm aresàx živòto im ne aresàx

But when we went to Holland I didn’t like their – I didn’t like their life.

134 (a) mnògo vodà da ìdex u dèckata gradìna decàta im žɤ̀lti kato

Too much water! We went to a kindergarten, their children are yellow like –

135 (a) ne mòg da ti gi kàžem ispìeni decà nè kato nàšte

I can’t describe them to you Washed-out children, not like ours,

136 (a) da sà ùbavi decà nè nèma ùbavi decà a jà im kàžex

[who] are beautiful children. No, there’s no beautiful children [there]. I told them

138 (a) u vòda živèja tì ne išlà li si tàm da vìiš kakvò e

They live in water. Have you gone there to see what it’s like?
