pickles

Bansko

70 (a) i sus kàžel' ftasàl turšìja

and with fermented cabbage, pickles.

74 (a) što sè kàzva cveklò cveklò što pràvaa zaxartà

what’s called beets. Beets, that they make sugar ...

76 (a) ud nègo ta nìj gu pràvime na turšìja

... out of. Well, we make pickles out of it.

Izgrev/Car

15 (a) də ədème š’à: svər’ɤ̀t fasùl’ ə pupèr turšìe kukòški vər’àhə

for us to eat. They’d cook up beans, peppers, pickles – and they cooked chicken –

Vladimirovo 2

68 (b) sɤs kìselo zèle ili turšìjka nàj naprède

… with sauerkraut, or pickles [as the] first course ...
