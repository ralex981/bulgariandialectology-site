beets

Bansko

71 (RSh) a štò e tavà č'ekàn'

And what’s this “chekan”?

72 (a) emi kà də ti kàža cveklò

Hm, how can I tell you? It’s beets –

74 (a) što sè kàzva cveklò cveklò što pràvaa zaxartà

what’s called beets. Beets, that they make sugar ...

76 (a) ud nègo ta nìj gu pràvime na turšìja

... out of. Well, we make pickles out of it.

85 (a) kàžel' i čekàn' i dròbine

… cabbage, and beets, and chunks of meat.

Mogilica 3

62 (a) zə fəsùl' enəkòf fəsùl'ef kuč'ɛ̀n gu zuvɛ̀hme

For beans. We called it a sort of “bean-beet.”

63 (a) š'ekèref kuč'ɛ̀n pò rànu ne sɛ̀hme sètne z'ɔ̀hme tegàj də sɛ̀eme

We didn't used to plant sugar beets. Then we bought [some] in order to plant them.

64 (a) tegàvə petmès ud nègə iskàrvəhme zàhərnu nəkvò beše

Then we made molasses from them; it was sugary, you know.

65 (VZh) a petmès kàk se prài petmès

Ah, molasses. How do you make molasses?

66 (a) č'e kuč'ɛ̀nən gu zə iskàrəš' ud nìvətə

You bring the beets in from the field,

67 (a) i ubɛ̀liš' gu hùbəvu i f ədìn kəzàn gu svərìš'

you peel them nicely, and and you boil them up in a cauldron.

68 (a) i tegà s prèsə gu se iscədì i tugàvə gu vər

Then it's strained through a press and then you –

69 (a) kətu gu izvər'ɔ̀t f ədnɔ̀ tòvə i stàne petmès

They turn it out into a pan and it becomes molasses.

Vŭrbina 4

9 (d) i jə s’ɛ̀eme pu nèjə sɛ̀eme i kučɛ̀ni kərtòfi fəsùl’

and we sow it [then]. Next to it we sow beets, potatoes, beans,
