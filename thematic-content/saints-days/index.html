Vladimirovo 1

102 (a) vɤž a a lovìli sme ngi dèn elì na gèrg’ovden

You see, we “caught” a saint’s day for them – either St. George’s day,

103 (a) elì na ə spàsovden elì na svetì ilìja elì na drùgi dène

or the Savior’s day, or St. Elias’s day, or some other days,

104 (a) kòlko pràznici ìma mòo go narečèš nàj màlko na trì pràznici

however many there are. You have to pick at least three holidays [for candidates].

110 (a) i gi zapàlime i detèto kojò fàne tavà na tòo

and light them. And whichever candle the child grabs for, that’s the one

111 (a) i go narečème dnèska gèrg’ovden zaràn

that we name as his saint’s day. Today St. George’s day, tomorrow –

112 (b) velìgden

Easter.

113 (a) spàsovden velìgden

Ascension, Easter,

114 (b) dimìtrovden

St. Dimitri’s day.
