thorns

Baskalci 1

241 (a) à tùka is trɤ̀nite is pàproteka kàk òdɤt tò okàpva

when they move around hereabouts among thorns and ferns, it falls off.

Tŭrnjane 1

2 (a) štò e imàlo mnògo tṛ̀n’e

Because there used to be a lot of thorns.

3 (ED) ama koè ka kakvò tr̀n’e

But which – what kind of thorns?

14 (a) i tṛ̀n’e e imàlo tùk po trotoàrete nasèkɤde tie

And there were thorns there along the sidewalks, everywhere these –

15 (a) i às pòmnim kogà e bilò tṛ̀n’eto

Even I remember when there were thorns [here].

16 (ED) [laughter] ama tò ə nalì tṛ̀n’e kato ž ә

[laughter] But this – isn’t [a matter of] thorns like …

18 (ED) takòva dràka

… the one that’s called Jerusalem thorn?

19 (a) nè nè è nalì ìma ednò bàbin zɤ̀p ìma ednò

No, no. There’s this – caltrop, you know? This –

21 (a) takɤ̀f tekìvi nìski trɤ̀ni

This – these low-lying thorns.

23 (a) a nè e dràkata i tìja

That’s not Jerusalem thorn.

25 (a) i sә go kṛstìle tṛn’àne

And so they called [the village] Thorn-place.
